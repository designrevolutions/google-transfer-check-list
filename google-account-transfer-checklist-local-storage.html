Google transfer checklist

Move everything safely

Work through Gmail, Google Drive and Google Photos one at a time. Tick each section when you have confirmed the data exists in the receiving account.

  1. Before starting, log into the old/source Google Workspace Gmail account.
  2. Go to Gmail > Settings > See all settings > Forwarding and POP/IMAP.
  3. Under POP Download, choose Enable POP for all mail, then click Save changes.
  4. In the receiving Gmail account, go to Settings > Accounts and Import.
  5. Choose Check mail from other accounts or Import mail and contacts, depending on what Gmail shows you.
  6. When asked for POP details, use POP server: pop.gmail.com, Port: 995, and enable SSL.
  7. For the username, use the full old email address, for example name@yourdomain.co.uk.
  8. If the normal password does not work, create a Google App Password and use that instead.
  9. Check that important labels, sent mail, attachments and old conversations are present.
  10. Keep a Google Takeout backup before deleting anything from the old account.

App password notes

App passwords are not shown as a main left-side menu item in Google Account. Open the old/source account, go to Security & sign-in, then search the Google Account page for App passwords. You can also try going directly to https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.

If App passwords is missing, first make sure 2-Step Verification is fully enabled. If it is still missing, the Google Workspace admin may have blocked app passwords, Advanced Protection may be enabled, or you may be signed into the wrong Google account.

When you create the app password, name it something like Gmail POP import, then paste the 16-character password into the Gmail import password field.

  1. Put the files into one main folder in the source Google Drive account.
  2. Share that folder with the receiving account as Editor.
  3. In the receiving account, open Shared with me and copy the files into My Drive.
  4. Confirm ownership and storage before removing files from the source account.
  1. In the source account, open Google Photos and enable Partner Sharing.
  2. Invite the receiving Google account and share all photos, or photos from a chosen date.
  3. In the receiving account, accept the invite and choose Save to your account > All photos.
  4. Check old, recent and video items before deleting anything from the source account.

Tip: do not delete anything from the old account until the receiving account has been checked carefully. A Takeout backup is useful as a final safety net.
