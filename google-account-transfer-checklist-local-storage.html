Google transfer checklist

Move everything safely

Work through Email, Google Drive and Google Photos separately. Each individual step has its own tick box, and your ticks are saved in this browser.

Email transfer

Use Gmail POP import for the old Google Workspace mailbox, then check that important mail has arrived.

Email progress

App password notes

If App passwords is missing, first make sure 2-Step Verification is fully enabled. If it is still missing, the Google Workspace admin may have blocked app passwords, Advanced Protection may be enabled, or you may be signed into the wrong Google account.

Gmail POP import may pause and continue later. For large mailboxes, it can take hours or longer.

Google Drive transfer

For shared folders, do not rely on dragging into My Drive. Use download/upload for real copies, or copy individual Google files where possible.

Drive progress

Drive transfer note

For normal files, download and upload is usually the simplest safe method. For Google-native files, copying individual files may preserve them better than downloading and converting them.

Google Photos transfer

Use Partner Sharing where possible, then make sure the receiving account is saving the shared photos into its own library.

Photos progress

Google Photos note

If you clicked Yes when Google asked whether to auto-save all shared photos, that is most likely the correct action. The next step is to verify by checking old photos, recent photos and videos inside the receiving account.

Because the old/source account had Pixel-related photo storage benefits, some photos may not have counted against storage in the old account. When those photos are saved into the new account, they may start counting against the new account’s storage.

Setting the new/receiving account to Storage saver is a sensible step, but you should still watch for Google storage warnings and check the storage usage after the photo transfer has had time to settle.

Keep the Takeout backup until you have checked the receiving account properly. This is especially important for videos, screenshots, WhatsApp images, downloaded images and app-folder photos.

Tip: do not delete anything from the old account until the receiving account has been checked carefully. A Takeout backup is useful as a final safety net.
